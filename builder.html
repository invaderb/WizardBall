Drag = draw the selected thing: wall, slow-mo zone (grey), or a goal. Ramp / Quarter-pipe: drag TOWARD the tall end (drag left-to-right for a tall right side, right-to-left for a tall left side). "Goal P1" is where player 1 scores; you can add as many as you like. Right-click or Erase tool = delete the wall under the cursor. Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo. Walls: a straight drag makes a 2px hairline wall (to line up with a ramp's edge); otherwise they grow in 8px steps, and you can drag an edge in to make a wall as thin as 2px. Snapping: drawing, resizing and moving snap onto the edges of other objects (dashed pink guides show which), so walls and ramps line up exactly; a hairline wall sits flush on the side your cursor is on. Hold Alt/Option to turn that off and use the plain 8px grid. Arrow keys nudge the selection 1px (Shift: 8px). Stamps place on click. Erase a piece of the left/right boundary wall to make a warp gap. Move tool: drag the ball spawn, a player spawn (colored pill), a player's health bar (3 colored segments), a wall, a goal (solid color box) or a slow-mo zone (dashed box). Grab a wall, goal or zone by its edge or corner (cursor turns into a resize arrow) to resize it. Keys and colors are still edited in the JSON below, then press Load.

Select: Move tool (M), click a wall, ramp, goal or zone (cyan outline). Anything you draw is selected for you. Duplicate Ctrl/Cmd+D · Copy / Paste Ctrl/Cmd+C / V (pastes at the cursor) · Flip F (turns a ramp around) · Mirror Shift+F (moves it across the arena's center line) · Grass G (on/off for a selected wall; wide walls get it automatically) · Goal particles: the Off / Left / Right / Top / Bottom buttons pick which side of the selected goal (box or circle) bubbles drift out of; new goals start facing the middle of the arena; S cycles them · Delete Del · Deselect Esc · Tools: W wall, E erase, M move.
